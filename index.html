Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is EasyFitness?

    EasyFitness is a subscription-based fitness content service that helps users stay active and manage their fitness routine. The service includes nutrition and calorie tracking, training programs of different complexity, and a library of exercises from professional trainers.

  • How do I subscribe?

    To subscribe, enter your mobile number and confirm the PIN code sent by SMS. After confirmation, your subscription will be activated on your mobile account.

  • How much does the service cost?

    Standard subscription charges apply depending on your mobile operator. Pricing includes: 0.7 KWD per week for Zain users, 0.2 KWD/day for STC users, 0.8 KWD per week for prepaid users, and 3.5 KWD per month for postpaid Ooredoo users (VAT inclusive where applicable).

  • Does the subscription renew automatically?

    Yes. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it.

  • How can I cancel my subscription?

    You can unsubscribe at any time using the contact email shown on the page or by sending the operator-specific stop command:
    - Zain: UNSUB EF to 93052
    - STC: STOP2 to 50365
    - Ooredoo: STOP 1 to 1371

  • Is my payment information secure?

    Yes. Subscription charges are processed through your mobile operator billing flow, and confirmation is completed by SMS PIN verification.
